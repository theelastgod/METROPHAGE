# Solana

$METRO lives on **Solana**. It launches on **pump.fun** (a bonding curve, then **PumpSwap**). The game never mints the token — the treasury is a holder, same as you. In-game credits (₵) stay off-chain; only deposits, cash-outs, and **Genesis Keys** touch the chain.

Sign-in is a free wallet signature. You do not need SOL to play.

## Wallets

Connect **Phantom, Solflare, or Backpack**.

* **Sign-in** is a free message signature. No SOL.
* **Desktop** — the wallet extension signs in the browser.
* **Phone** — pick the wallet, approve in the app, return to this tab. Play stays in the browser.

Your runner binds to that Solana address. A **guest** is a [device save](getting-started.md) until you link the wallet — then the same callsign cashes out and can hold a Key.

## SOL gas

* **Deposits** — you pay a little **SOL as gas**. **$METRO** is what fills the pool. Keep a scrap of SOL in the wallet or the transfer won't land.
* **Cash-outs** — the **treasury pays SOL**. It will also open your $METRO token account (an **ATA**) the first time you cash out. If treasury SOL is gone, cash-outs stay closed until it is refilled.
* You do **not** need SOL to sign in, to play, or to cash out.

## pump.fun & PumpSwap

$METRO is created on **pump.fun**, not by the game. Until it graduates, price discovery is the curve; after graduation it trades on **PumpSwap**. Deposits and withdrawals are ordinary token transfers either way.

The Metro panel shows the **cluster** (devnet or mainnet) and the **mint**. Copy the mint exactly — Solana addresses are case-sensitive.

## Play, then convert

Playing is the only faucet. Earn ₵ in the city; convert to $METRO only when the [player-funded pool](economy.md) can cover it. Complete a cash-out claim quickly (~2 minutes) or ₵ comes back. Empty pool, empty treasury SOL, or a tripped oracle → **"Check back later."**

## Genesis Keys

The 50 homes on THE ESTATES are **Genesis Key** NFTs. Holding the Key **is** the deed. Furniture, guestbook, and the ₵ listing stay in the city; the door belongs to whoever holds the NFT. Unsold Keys sit with the treasury until someone buys one in-game. Link a wallet first — guests can walk the street, not take a Key. Details in [City Systems](systems.md).
